# AKP Tech - Contributor Guidance

## How to Apply

1. Explore our GSoC Ideas List
2. Choose a project that matches your skills
3. Contact mentors via GitHub or email

---

## Proposal Guidelines

A good proposal should include:

- Introduction about yourself
- Project you are interested in
- Clear understanding of the problem
- Proposed solution and approach
- Timeline (week-by-week plan)
- Past experience (if any)

---

## Tips for Success

- Keep your proposal clear and structured
- Show your problem-solving approach
- Contribute to our repositories before applying
- Communicate actively with mentors
